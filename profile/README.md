## What is DxO PhotoLab?

**DxO PhotoLab** is a powerful, professional-grade photo editing application renowned for its superior image quality and advanced, AI-driven correction tools. It stands out in the digital photography workflow by offering unparalleled optical corrections, leveraging scientifically measured data from thousands of camera and lens combinations. Photographers trust DxO PhotoLab for its exceptional noise reduction technology, **DeepPRIME XD**, which uses artificial intelligence to remove noise and recover detail from RAW files with stunning results, even in extremely low-light conditions.

Beyond noise reduction, the software provides a comprehensive suite of tools for adjusting lighting, contrast, color, and sharpness. Its **U Point technology**, inherited from the legendary Nik Collection, allows for precise local adjustments with control points, making complex edits simple and intuitive. Whether you are a professional photographer demanding the utmost quality from your images or an enthusiast looking to elevate your photography, DxO PhotoLab offers a non-destructive, fluid, and powerful editing experience that integrates seamlessly into any workflow.

## 📚 Quick Start Guide

1. Download and install **DxO PhotoLab** from the official website.
2. Launch the application and import your photos, especially RAW files.
3. Discover automatic corrections in the **Customize** tab.
4. Use **DeepPRIME XD** for dramatic noise reduction on high-ISO images.
5. Apply local adjustments with **U Point** technology for fine-tuning.
6. Export your final image in your desired format and quality.
